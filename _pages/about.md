I am an ESRC-funded doctoral student in Politics at the University of Edinburgh and an affiliate postgraduate researcher at the University of Glasgow. My project is entitled "Understanding the Multi-Level Voter with Evidence from the Scottish Election Study. I am supervised by Professor Ailsa Henderson and Professor Christopher Carman.

Before my PhD I worked in the States of Guernsey Civil Service, in the Strategy and Policy department, supporting the island's principal committees. I currently teach as a Graduate Teaching Assistant at the University of Glasgow, and in spring 2026 I worked as a backroom psephologist on the BBC's Election 26 programme covering the Scottish Parliament Election.

See my [publications](/publications/) and [CV](/cv/) for more information about my recent work.

In my spare time, I enjoy listening to good music, rock climbing, watching films, travelling and whitewater kayaking.
