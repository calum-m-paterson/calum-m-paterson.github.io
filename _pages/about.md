I am an ESRC-funded doctoral student in Politics at the University of Edinburgh and an affiliate postgraduate researcher at the University of Glasgow. I work with the Scottish Election Study on the changing and multi-level electorate of Scotland.

Before my PhD I worked in the States of Guernsey Civil Service, in the Strategy and Policy department, supporting the island's principal committees. I currently teach as a Graduate Teaching Assistant at the University of Glasgow, and in spring 2026 I worked as a psephologist on the BBC's Election 26 programme.

My research under review, with John Garry, Ailsa Henderson and Richard Wyn Jones, examines how low state identification relates to support for secession in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. See my [publications](/publications/) and [CV](/cv/) for more.

In my spare time, I enjoy listening to good music, rock climbing, watching films, travelling and whitewater kayaking.
